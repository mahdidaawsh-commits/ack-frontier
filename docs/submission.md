Title: AckFrontier: Consensus bounded-response monitor

Description:

AckFrontier monitors alarm-response obligations across ordered prose checkpoint logs. Leader and validators independently fetch commit-pinned reports, verify exact bytes and derive four YES/NO/UNKNOWN event atoms. Validators compare every decision and audit substantive source anchors, distinguishing actual human acknowledgements from transport receipts, promises and historical events. A bounded frontier propagates all Boolean interpretations of uncertainty through a temporal monitor. Repeated alarms cannot reset the oldest deadline; an exact-deadline acknowledgement succeeds; a late acknowledgement cannot erase a breach. Results distinguish CLEAR, PENDING, AT_RISK and VIOLATED, and exhausting logs does not close pending obligations. The repository includes independent whole-history Python/JavaScript oracles, explicit validator tests and a CLI proof runner. Synthetic reports demonstrate documentary checkpoint semantics, not physical service delivery or wall-clock response time.
